\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

Architectural mechanisms in language models are typically introduced with a presumed contribution to the optimisation objective: a new attention variant, normalisation, or gating scheme is judged by whether it lowers the loss. We instead investigate a setting in which an architectural channel is free to develop structured internal representations \emph{without} measurably affecting the loss, and ask what such a channel does.

Our setting is built on hybrid state-space/attention language models~\citep{gu2023mamba,vaswani2017attention,lieber2024jamba,glorioso2024zamba,park2024empirical}. Inside this backbone we introduce \emph{hormone routing}, a parameter-efficient channel that injects a token-conditional gated combination of a small bank of frozen direction vectors into the residual stream after each attention block. The bank is non-trainable; the router and gate are. The mechanism adds under $0.01\%$ of base parameters and is gated such that, at initialisation, the modification is an exact no-op and the hybrid backbone is recovered bit-for-bit. Directions are obtained from a partially-trained Stage-1 checkpoint via document-style contrast-pair extraction~\citep{turner2023activation,rimsky2024steering,zou2023representation}.

\paragraph{The empirical phenomenon.} Across every hormone-routing variant we tested at the $125$M-parameter, $2$B-token scale --- with extracted directions, with random unit vectors, with the gate frozen at $1.0$, and with directions extracted from a randomly-initialised model --- the router converges to a strongly genre-discriminative routing distribution (max pairwise $L_1$ distance across six text genres: $0.6$--$1.6$, vs $0.0$ for uniform). The specialisation is qualitatively interpretable in the variant where directions carry semantic labels (news $\to$ urgency, fiction $\to$ curiosity, code $\to$ skepticism) and structurally analogous in the variants where they do not. Yet none of these variants moves the validation perplexity needle: all properly-initialised variants achieve identical val PPL ($26.13$), and loss trajectories trace the same curve to within $10^{-3}$ across all of Stage 2. Training the same router from scratch with hormones in place from step $0$ damages the loss by $+2.07$ PPL even though it still develops similar specialisation. \textbf{The loss-relevant variable is when the perturbation is introduced, not what it contains.}

\paragraph{Disambiguating the regime.} Two readings remain open: either the perturbation magnitude in the unforced variants is too small to matter (capacity-bottlenecked), or the architecture has actively absorbed it (structural homeostasis). We disambiguate with a sixth variant with a forced non-zero gate initialisation and a non-trainable $3\times$ amplification --- producing an effective perturbation $\approx 2\times$ the largest unforced gate. The model drifts the gates downward and shrinks per-hormone magnitudes but cannot escape because $\alpha$ is non-trainable; the residual perturbation it cannot suppress does not damage validation loss at the matched training step. The capacity-bottlenecked reading would predict improvement; the intrinsically-harmful reading would predict damage; we observe neither. We refer to the regime within which the gated injection is consistently absorbed without measurable change to predictions as a \emph{homeostatic envelope}.

\paragraph{Contributions.}
\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Empirical (primary)}: a clean decoupling between routing-channel specialisation and downstream loss in a 125M-parameter hybrid LM. Five hormone-routing variants on a 2B-token budget all develop strongly genre-discriminative routing; specialisation strength does not correlate with validation perplexity. A sixth, forced-engagement variant rules out the capacity-bottlenecked reading. The only loss-relevant axis is training schedule.
    \item \textbf{Architectural (primary)}: \emph{hormone routing}, a token-conditional softmax-gated routing channel, plus a document-style contrast-pair extraction protocol that recovers behavioural directions from the base model itself.
\end{itemize}

We treat this as future work rather than a claim of the paper: full-budget evaluation requires a fused CUDA kernel that is out of scope here.
