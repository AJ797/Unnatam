\begin{abstract}
We construct a controlled setting in which an architectural channel in a hybrid state-space/attention language model is free to organise its internal representations without measurably affecting the optimisation objective. The channel is \emph{hormone routing}: a token-conditional softmax-gated linear combination of a small bank of frozen direction vectors, added to the residual stream after each attention block. We pretrain a 125M-parameter Mamba/MQA hybrid on FineWeb-Edu under a fixed 2B-token budget across five variants of this channel --- extracted directions, random unit vectors, vectors extracted from a randomly-initialised model, and a frozen gate at $1.0$. The router develops semantically-coherent per-genre routing specialisation in every variant, with maximum pairwise $L_1$ distance between genre routing distributions (at the engaged upper attention layer) in $0.96$--$1.56$. Controls on the \textsc{HR-forced} checkpoint over $20$ held-out documents per genre confirm this: the observed $L_1 = 1.16$ is $\approx 2.5\times$ the document-level permutation-null 99th percentile ($0.47$) and $\approx 15\times$ larger than an untrained-router baseline ($0.076$), so the specialisation is not an artefact of passage selection nor a projection of input-side differences alone (Section~\ref{sec:analysis}). The actual residual perturbation magnitude is small: $\|\Delta h\|_2 / \|h\|_2 < 0.2\%$ across attention layers, even under $2\times$-amplified forced engagement. The variant of \emph{highest} specialisation strength is the one whose gate the model cannot modulate. \textbf{Specialisation strength does not predict downstream loss}: the four properly-initialised variants converge to identical validation perplexity ($26.13$). To probe whether this loss-invariance is a ceiling or a regime the model actively absorbs, a sixth variant adds a forced non-zero gate initialisation and a non-trainable $3\times$ amplification. The model drifts the gates downward and shrinks the per-hormone magnitudes, but the residual perturbation it cannot suppress --- $\approx 2\times$ the unforced regime --- does not damage loss either. We refer to the regime in which the gated injection is consistently absorbed without affecting predictions as a \emph{homeostatic envelope}. The envelope is wider than the unforced variants naturally select; the axis along which we observe measurable loss damage is the training schedule (introducing routing during base pretraining rather than as a continuation raises validation perplexity by $+2.07$ PPL). An isolation ablation reported in Section~\ref{subsec:noext-cont} runs the same random-init-extracted bank as a staged continuation and finds it recovers most of the way to Base: the observed damage splits between the two tested conditions as $\approx 1.64$ PPL from the schedule change (the from-scratch-vs-continuation delta at fixed bank) and $\approx 0.43$ PPL from the bank change (the extracted-vs-random-init-extracted delta at fixed staged schedule). This is a descriptive comparison between two observed conditions rather than a fully identified crossed causal decomposition (a from-scratch-with-extracted-good-bank arm would be required for the latter; we leave it to future work, Section~\ref{sec:analysis}). All results are at a single scale ($125$M parameters, $2$B tokens, one architecture family, single seed per variant); we hedge accordingly. Appendix~\ref{app:ia} sketches a second architectural mechanism --- intracellular attention, slot attention inside the SSM scan --- as future work.
\end{abstract}
