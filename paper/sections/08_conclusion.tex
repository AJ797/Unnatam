\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We constructed a controlled setting in which an architectural channel in a hybrid state-space/attention language model is free to organise its internal representations without measurably affecting the optimisation objective. The channel --- \emph{hormone routing}, a token-conditional softmax-gated combination of frozen direction vectors added to the residual stream --- adds under $0.01\%$ trainable parameters and is gated such that the unmodified hybrid backbone is recovered exactly at initialisation.

The router develops semantically-coherent per-genre routing specialisation across every variant we examined: extracted directions, random unit vectors, vectors extracted from a randomly-initialised model, and a frozen gate at $1.0$ all yield layer-$11$ max pairwise $L_1$ distance between genre routing distributions in the range $0.96$--$1.56$, well above a permutation-null 99th percentile of $0.62$. The variant of \emph{highest} specialisation strength is one whose gate the model cannot modulate. Yet at $125$M parameters and $2$B tokens, none of this organisation produces a measurable contribution to the loss objective: all properly-initialised variants converge to identical validation perplexity. \textbf{Specialisation strength does not correlate with loss.} A forced-engagement variant with non-trainable $3\times$ amplification on the gated injection drifts the gates downward but does not damage validation loss either, ruling out both the capacity-bottlenecked reading and the intrinsically-harmful reading. We refer to the bounded regime in which the gated injection is consistently absorbed without affecting predictions as the architecture's \emph{homeostatic envelope}. The envelope is wider than the unforced variants naturally select; the axis along which we observe measurable loss damage is the training schedule. An isolation ablation (Section~\ref{subsec:noext-cont}) runs the same random-init-extracted bank as a staged continuation and shows it recovers most of the way to Base ($26.56$ PPL vs $26.13$). The observed $+2.07$ PPL total damage of the original \textsc{HR-noext} splits, across the two tested conditions, into $\approx 1.64$ PPL associated with the schedule change and $\approx 0.43$ PPL with the bank change; a full causal decomposition would require the crossed from-scratch-with-extracted-good-bank arm, which we do not run and leave to future work (Section~\ref{sec:analysis}). All results are at a single scale (125M parameters, 2B tokens, one architecture family, one seed per variant).

\paragraph{Future directions.} The routing-organisation-without-loss-relevance finding should be tested at larger scale: whether the homeostatic envelope's width scales with $\dmodel$ or saturates determines whether the bounded-engagement regime we observe is a fundamental property of the mechanism or a capacity-bottlenecked phenomenon. The hormone-extraction protocol could be made continuous, updating $V$ on a slow schedule throughout training. Appendix~\ref{app:ia} sketches a second architectural mechanism (intracellular attention, slot attention inside the SSM scan) whose full evaluation requires a fused CUDA kernel; we treat it as future work. A broader research programme suggested by this work is the systematic study of \emph{introspective architectural channels}: components that develop structured internal organisation along axes the architecture itself defines, observable to interpretation but decoupled --- at least at small scale --- from the optimisation objective.
